Neckbiters — Bite a Neck Spirits

The life of an immigrant can be frustrating in so many ways. Particularly when you're overwhelmed by nostalgia, and you'd want to "taste home" again, that black liquor with the red star that used to flow in your veins. Unfortunately, that liquor doesn't exist in this here foreign land.

It is from necessity that the best projects arise, the urgency to provide for a niche need; when you don't have the money you have to roll up your sleeves and DO IT YOURSELF.

And so the first Bite A Neck spirit was created: Coffee Bomb - Batch #1, a full and creamy espresso coffee liquor, that tastes like home. After that first success, pulled in by a strong will to experiment and the not so splendid economical situation, the first chocolate liquor variants were born, tones of mint and chili, followed by the first fruit infusions, trying my best to always adhere to the strictest DIY ethic: whether it be foraging in the mountains, appropriating fruit in gardens and places were fruit is uncontaminated, those fruits that modern marketing would brand "bio".

Each and every bottle contains tracks of an adventure, usually lived in good company, the support of family and friends blended in each drop, the forbearing experimentation that leads to unique and one of a kind results, a growth whose movement can only be impeded by time, the greatest barrier, to be climbed armed with patience, good music and the warmth of a hug.

Time that at the same time becomes a resource, teaches, punishes each mistake and allows to perfect the liquor and yourself, in a neverending chase that mirrors our life philosophy flawlessly.

Ours because, with the first sip, with the first bottle that you gift and obviously share, you end up becoming part of my life, contributing with a substantial push to the next batch. And who knows, maybe one day we'll stop together to breathe in an air that smells like change, enjoying a glass that tastes like sharing, mutual understanding and that concept that is as human as it is sacred: never take a step back.

Heartfelt thanks from Giorgio, you knew that already tho, right?
